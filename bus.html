A dining room that used to do the school run

The seats came out and a long table went in. What's left is a warm, close room where everyone can hear each other and watch the plates come together a few feet away.

It suits the dinners that deserve more than a restaurant booking: a milestone birthday, an anniversary, a proposal, or a team you want to thank properly.

Two ways to ride

We come to you

We park at your home, office or venue and dinner happens right outside your door. All we need is a level place to park and a little notice.

What we need on site

You come to us

Meet the bus at one of our preferred locations around Seattle. We pick the spot for the view and the setting; you just bring your guests.

A sample evening

Four courses, one from each stop on our route. The menu changes with the season.

  • Smoked salmon on rye New York

    Wild salmon, caraway rye crisp, cultured cream, dill.

  • Hand-cut tagliatelle Italy

    Foraged mushrooms, brown butter, thyme. Vegetarian.

  • Roasted king salmon Pacific Northwest

    Beurre blanc, leeks, fingerling potatoes. Gluten-free.

  • Fruit tart Paris

    Pâte sucrée, vanilla pastry cream, whatever fruit is best that week.
