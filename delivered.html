Delivered catering

Lunch boxes, platters, breakfast and sweets, dropped off ready to serve.

How delivery works

  1. Choose your menu

    Pick from the menu below, or tell us the headcount and budget and we'll suggest a spread.

  2. Send your order

    Give us the date, time, address and any dietary needs. We'll confirm with a total.

  3. We drop it off

    Your order arrives labelled and ready to serve, with everything you need to set it out.
