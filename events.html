Event catering

We write the menu, cook on site and serve your guests, so you can be one of them.

Events we cater

  • Nonprofit and school fundraisers

    From small celebrations to gala events.

  • Company events

    Client dinners, launches, offsites and holiday parties, fully staffed.

  • Private dinners

    A coursed meal cooked in your kitchen for birthdays, anniversaries or no reason at all.

  • Celebrations

    Showers, graduations, memorials and family gatherings of any size.

Every menu is written for the event

Tell us the date, the headcount and what you love to eat. We'll send back a menu and a quote, then adjust until it's right. Dietary needs are part of the plan from the start.
